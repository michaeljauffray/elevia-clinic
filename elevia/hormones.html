Questions, answered

Is the consult really free?
Yes — completely. The 10-minute discovery call costs nothing and there's no obligation to continue. It exists so we can both work out whether Elevia is the right fit for you. If you choose to proceed, a full initial consultation is $187.
My blood tests came back "normal". Can you still help?
This is the most common situation we see. Standard testing often misses subtle hormonal imbalances — especially during perimenopause, when hormones fluctuate day to day. We assess the full picture, not just a single snapshot.
Do I need to visit a clinic in person?
No. Elevia Clinic is 100% telehealth and serves women Australia-wide. Consults happen by phone or video, and any testing is arranged near you.
Do you prescribe hormone therapy?
Where appropriate, yes — including bioidentical hormone therapy. We combine evidence-based conventional medicine with natural, holistic approaches, and every plan is personalised to you.
What happens after the free call?
If we believe we can help, you can book a full initial consultation ($187) where we take a comprehensive history and design your personalised testing and treatment plan. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you honestly.
